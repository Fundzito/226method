The Fun Page - Life, Nights Out & Social Eating

Life isn't just workouts and meals. The 226 Method shows you how to enjoy nights out, family dinners, and social events while still staying on track.

Drinking Smart & Staying Mindful

  • Swap mixed drinks for spritzers or shots.
  • Choose wine over dark liquor for fewer calories and easier control.
  • Limit intake: max 2 drinks or 4 shots.
  • Pace yourself and alternate with water to stay hydrated.
  • It takes roughly 30 minutes of exercise to burn ~300 calories, but only 30 seconds to consume it. Be mindful: the speed at which you see results depends on your choices.
  • Most of all, enjoy your time with loved ones, family, or your significant other. Consistency wins every time.
226 Pro Tip
You don't have to completely avoid alcohol or indulgence to stick to the 226 Method. Be intentional, stay hydrated, pace yourself, and remember your long-term goals. Life is for enjoying, not stressing.

226 Pro Tip

Eating out doesn't mean giving up control. Fill most of your plate with protein and veggies, enjoy your carbs in moderation, and stick to a portion you can finish comfortably. This keeps social meals fun without derailing progress.
