The Kitchen - High-Gain Fuel

Morning Ritual - ACV Fat-Burner

Kickstart digestion, metabolism, and fat-burning benefits.

1-2 tbsp raw Apple Cider Vinegar

1 tsp raw honey

1 tsp fresh grated ginger (or 1/2 tsp ginger powder)

1/4 tsp cayenne pepper

8-12 oz water

Instructions

  1. Mix ingredients in water.
  2. Drink on an empty stomach, 15-30 min before breakfast.
  3. Optional: pinch of cinnamon for flavor and blood sugar support.
  4. Rinse mouth after drinking to protect enamel.
Tips & Alternatives
  • Start with 1 tbsp ACV if new to it.
  • Honey is optional for lower-calorie preference.
  • Ginger powder works if fresh ginger isn’t available.
  • Cayenne can be reduced or increased to taste.

The 226 Grocery List - Budget Muscle Foods

You don't need expensive supplements or fancy health foods to build strength. If you can afford them, go for it-but they aren't necessary. I use some of the pricier options myself, usually when they're on sale, but I'm also diligent about finding cheaper alternatives. Pro tip: always compare the ingredients. Sometimes the budget option has exactly what you need, and you're just paying extra for added ingredients or branding with the more expensive one. The 226 Method focuses on simple, affordable foods that deliver high protein, energy, and the nutrients your body needs to grow and recover.

The 226 Plate Rule

One of the easiest ways to stay on track is to build your meals around a simple plate structure. Instead of counting every calorie, focus on what your plate looks like.

In the 226 Method, vegetables and protein should make up about 70-80% of your plate. Carbohydrates fill the remaining portion to provide energy for training and recovery.

You don't need perfect measurements. Just build your plate with mostly protein and vegetables, and you'll naturally stay on track.

The key to the 226 Method is consistency. Stock your kitchen with foods that make it easier to stay disciplined every day.
